Payments & Clearing Analytics

Three interactive dashboards for card processing, transaction mix and clearing reconciliation · H1 2026 · synthetic data

Card Transactions Overview

Volume, amount, average ticket, success rate and commission by card type (UZCARD, HUMO, VISA, Mastercard, UnionPay), with period-over-period comparison.

Transaction Types & Processing Costs

Purchase, P2P, cash and wallet mix; processing cost by acquiring bank; commission income vs cost and net fee margin.

Clearing & Reconciliation Monitoring

Clearing account balances, positive/negative positions, payment-gateway file status, daily reconciliation gaps, error reasons and alerts.

All figures are generated by scripts/build_dashboard_data.py (seed 42). The same data is available as CSV fact tables in data/powerbi/ for Power BI.
